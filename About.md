SlateClub is a unified cinema platform designed as a “Spotify × Letterboxd for movies”—where discovery, tracking, and community exist in one place.

At its core is a strong, personalized recommendation engine that understands each user’s taste deeply—based on mood, tone, and storytelling style, not just genre—so every suggestion feels curated for you, not generic.

It also includes Letterboxd-style features like logging films, creating watchlists, rating, and writing reviews, 
allowing users to build a living profile of their cinematic taste over time.

Beyond that, SlateClub connects users through social discovery and discussions, and extends into real-world film events, turning it into more than just an app—a complete cinema ecosystem.

In short, it’s a platform where you can find, track, review, and experience films—all in one place.
