If you’re chasing the mood of Parasite rather than its nationality, look for films that combine:

* Social/class tension
* Dark humor
* Moral ambiguity
* Gradual escalation from normal → chaotic
* Suspense without being a conventional thriller

Here’s a broader mix from Hollywood, Korean cinema, Malayalam, Tamil, and elsewhere.

Must-Watch

The Menu (Hollywood)

The closest modern Hollywood equivalent.
Starts as a satire of wealth and privilege, then slowly turns into something much darker.

Burning

Probably the closest match in atmosphere.
Quiet, mysterious, uncomfortable, and full of class tension.

Triangle of Sadness

A brutal and hilarious takedown of the rich.
Very similar social satire energy.

⸻

Malayalam (Mollywood)

Joji

One of the strongest recommendations.
A wealthy family, greed, manipulation, and a protagonist making increasingly terrible choices.

Maheshinte Prathikaaram

Not a thriller, but has the same grounded social realism and layered characters.

Nayattu

Ordinary people trapped by a corrupt system.
The tension keeps building relentlessly.

Aattam

A single incident exposes class, gender, and power structures within a group.
Feels very much like Parasite in its social dissection.

⸻

Tamil (Kollywood)

Super Deluxe

Darkly funny, unpredictable, and full of social commentary.
One of India’s most unique films.

Jigarthanda

Crime, satire, black comedy, and escalating chaos.

Aaranya Kaandam

Dark, cynical, morally gray characters.
Cult classic.

Kuthiraivaal

More surreal, but carries that unsettling “something isn’t right” feeling.

⸻

Korean Cinema Beyond Parasite

The Handmaiden

Masterful deception and power struggles.

Mother

Starts small and spirals into something morally disturbing.

Memories of Murder

Dark humor mixed with tragedy and suspense.

⸻

Hollywood / International Gems

Nightcrawler

Watching an ambitious person become increasingly monstrous.

Get Out

Social commentary wrapped in suspense.

Ex Machina

Psychological tension and power dynamics.

The Talented Mr. Ripley

Class envy and identity manipulation.

Force Majeure

A tiny incident reveals ugly truths about people.

Wild Tales

Darkly comic stories about ordinary people snapping.

⸻

Hidden Gems That Capture the Same Feeling

* The Platform
* Coherence
* The Invitation
* Dogtooth
* Barton Fink
* Cache

My top 5 if you want the closest Parasite vibe:

1. Burning
2. The Menu
3. Joji
4. Triangle of Sadness
5. Aattam

These all have that signature feeling of watching seemingly ordinary people and situations unravel into something deeply uncomfortable, fascinating, and unpredictable.
